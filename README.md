## How I think about data

> Numbers tell you *where* to look. I got here from research -- so I care just as much about the *why* underneath them.

I started in operations, moved into data analysis, and got hooked on the gap between "the metric moved" and "here's what to actually do about it." That's the work I want more of -- dashboards and findings people actually use, not decks that sit in a drive folder.
